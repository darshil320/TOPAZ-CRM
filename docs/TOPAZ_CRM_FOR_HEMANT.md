# Your Topaz System — What It Is, In Plain Words

*A note for Hemant, from Darshil. Not a technical document — just what this thing
does for your business, day to day.*

---

## The problem this solves

Before this, Topaz ran the way most showrooms do: a customer's whole story — what
they liked, what they asked for, who they spoke to, what they still owe — lived in a
few different heads, a few WhatsApp threads, and a notebook or two. When a
salesperson was busy or on leave, that story went quiet. When a customer came back
three weeks later, nobody quite remembered what they wanted. When a piece was out
for polishing, nobody could say exactly where it was without calling around.

This system exists so that **nothing about a customer, an order, or a piece of
furniture depends on one person's memory.**

## What it actually does, start to finish

**At the door.** The camera recognises a face — new customer or someone who's been
in before — and pings the right salesperson on WhatsApp instantly. A new face pings
whoever's free to claim them. A returning face pings *their own* salesperson, by
name, with what that person wanted last time.

**While they're deciding.** Every visit, every note, every budget mentioned gets
saved against that customer automatically. If nobody follows up, the system nudges
the customer on WhatsApp — signed as their own salesperson, not as a robot — and any
salesperson can jump into that chat and take over any time.

**When they're ready to buy.** A salesperson builds a price quote right there on
their phone — item, size, price, discount — and the tax is worked out automatically,
never by hand. The customer gets a WhatsApp link, taps to approve. That approval
becomes an order by itself.

**Getting paid.** Every advance and instalment is recorded against the order. A
receipt goes out automatically. At any moment, you or your accounts person can see
exactly who owes what — no more digging through old bills or relying on memory.

**Making it.** Once an order's confirmed, every item's journey through your
workshops is tracked — design approved, cutting, frame work, polishing, finishing,
dispatch — with a photo taken at the steps that actually matter. If a piece needs to
go from one workshop to another (polish here, finish there), the system plans that
whole trip, gives it a deadline down to the hour, and hands it from one workshop to
the next by itself once the work's done — a driver gets a simple phone screen
telling them exactly what to collect, from where, and where it's going.

**Delivering it.** By the time it's ready, everyone involved — you, the
salesperson, the workshop, the driver — has already seen it coming, on time or
flagged if it's not.

## Why this actually helps the business grow

- **No lead goes cold.** Every walk-in, every Instagram/Facebook enquiry, gets
  captured and followed up automatically — not just the ones someone remembered to
  write down.
- **Faster follow-ups, more closes.** A customer who's thinking it over gets a
  message in your salesperson's own voice, at the right moment, without anyone
  having to remember to send it.
- **Fewer money mistakes.** Tax calculated by the system, not by hand. Payments
  recorded once, correctly, forever — nobody can accidentally take more than what's
  owed, and nobody can quietly forget a payment.
- **You see the whole shop from your phone.** Every customer, every order, every
  piece of furniture in production — one screen, without calling five people to ask
  "where is this at."
- **It scales with your team, not against it.** More salespeople, more workshops,
  more helpers on the floor — each person sees exactly what their job needs and
  nothing else. Nobody's waiting on one person who happens to remember everything.
- **Your reputation is protected.** A customer who was promised a delivery date
  gets exactly that, because the system is tracking it whether or not a human
  remembers to check.

## Built only for Topaz

This isn't an off-the-shelf CRM with your logo pasted on it. Every screen —
the way a salesperson claims a walk-in, the way your workshops hand work between
each other, the way a job card gets sent to Sharma's without a single rupee figure
leaking out — was built around **how Topaz actually works**, not how a generic
furniture business might. Nothing in it exists because "most CRMs have this" — it
exists because you asked for it, or because your actual daily work needed it.
